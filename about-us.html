About The Body Shop

The Body Shop is a private gym on Danbury Road in Wilton. Members get their own key and 24/7 access, which means the place is yours whenever you need it — the 4 a.m. lift before a shift, the late night after the kids are down, the holiday session when everywhere else is closed.

It’s a gritty gym, and we mean that as a compliment. Concrete, iron, and equipment that gets used hard and keeps working. What it isn’t is a big-box club with a sales floor, a waiting list for a rack, or a contract designed to be hard to leave.

The floor is built to cover everything. Free weights from light to heavy, racks and platforms, plate-loaded and selectorized machines, cardio, and the accessories that usually go missing at other gyms. Whatever you came in to train, you’ll find what you need to train it.

Our members are competitive athletes prepping for a meet, first responders coming off shift, high school athletes, college students home on break, moms getting a session in while the kids are at school, and people picking up a barbell for the first time at 50. Nobody here is out of place.

What holds it together is the members themselves. People know each other’s names, spot each other without being asked, and put their weights back. It’s tough and it’s friendly at the same time — a family atmosphere with heavy dumbbells in it.

Come see it. If it’s your kind of gym, you’ll know inside of 30 seconds.
